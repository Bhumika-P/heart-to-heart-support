Support Group Confidentiality Agreement

A shared commitment to a safe, private, and respectful environment.

Our group is committed to providing a safe, private, and respectful environment for everyone.

By participating in this group, I agree:

Confidentiality

What is shared in the group stays in the group. Members agree not to share another person's name, identity, story, personal circumstances, or anything discussed in the group with others.

Leader Exception

Group leaders may occasionally seek confidential guidance from a trusted professional, advisor, or experienced person when additional wisdom or assistance is needed to better support a member. Whenever possible, names and identifying details will be withheld, and only the information necessary to receive guidance will be shared.

Privacy Outside the Group

If I or co-leaders see a group member in public, we will not approach or acknowledge them unless they acknowledge us first. This protects anyone who may be with someone who does not know about their participation in the group.

Even if they acknowledge me or co-leaders first, we will not mention the group or anything discussed there unless they choose to do so.

Respect & Safety

Information learned through the group will never be used to gossip about, embarrass, threaten, manipulate, or harm another person.

I understand that violating these confidentiality guidelines may result in being asked to leave the group.

By signing below, I agree to respect these confidentiality and privacy guidelines.
