Your Information

Privacy Policy

How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Information you provide

When you RSVP, we collect your name, email address, and selected session. When you contact us, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message. We use this information to manage sessions, respond to you, and support group administration.

Who can access it

RSVPs and contact messages are accessible to authorized group administrators and are sent to the group’s private email inbox. Attendee lists are not displayed publicly. GitHub Pages hosts this website. Online forms are not connected yet. If the prepared Firebase integration is activated, Firebase will provide authentication and database services, and the selected email provider will process notification emails.

Website operation

Administrator sign-in uses browser storage for authentication. Public visitors do not need an account to RSVP. Hashed network identifiers and email-derived identifiers are used to limit repeated form submissions. Fonts load from Google Fonts. This website does not include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK.

Your choices

Share only the information you are comfortable sending. You can request correction or deletion of your RSVP or message through the Contact page. Administrators retain records for group administration until they are removed. Deleting a website record does not automatically delete copies already delivered to an email inbox.

Group confidentiality

The group confidentiality agreement describes members’ responsibilities and the limited leader-guidance exception.

Other websites

Book and music links open external websites, which have their own privacy practices.
