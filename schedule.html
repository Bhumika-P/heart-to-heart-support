Session Fee & Sponsorship

A $10 fee per session helps cover room rental, setup, and other expenses that keep our support group running.

If you would like to sponsor someone’s session fees or book costs, please contact Sherée directly or leave your sponsorship gift in the designated box at the entry of the meeting room.

If these costs are difficult for you to manage, please speak with Sherée privately. Someone on our sponsorship list may be able to help.
