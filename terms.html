Terms of Use

Please read these alongside the group confidentiality agreement.

Purpose of the group

Heart to Heart support groups provide education, peer support, encouragement, and coaching-based guidance. They are not psychotherapy or a substitute for diagnosis or treatment from a licensed mental-health professional.

Respectful participation

Participation is subject to the Support Group Confidentiality and Disclaimer Agreement. Violating its privacy and respect guidelines may result in being asked to leave the group.

Sessions and RSVPs

The schedule shows currently published session information. Please check the listed date, time, timezone, and location. If your plans change or you have questions about a session, contact Sherée.

Resources

Books and music linked from this site are resources for reflection and encouragement. External sites manage their own content, purchases, and availability.
