Help Someone Take Their Next Step Toward Healing

Financial hardship can make it difficult to access support when it is needed most. Your generosity can help sponsor coaching sessions, support group participation, or books and guided resources for someone who might otherwise be unable to afford them.

For some people experiencing emotional abuse, access to household money is restricted or controlled by their partner. Even when money is available in the home, they may be unable to use it to seek support. Sponsoring a session, support group participation, or a book can help remove a financial barrier to beginning their healing journey.

By helping provide a book, Transformation Guide, and Breakthrough Journal, you can give someone practical tools for reflection, understanding, and growth between sessions or as they begin exploring their healing journey.

Contributions also help with outreach and travel expenses, allowing Sherée to share faith-centered teaching about emotional healing with churches, ministries, and other interested groups.

Whether you sponsor a session, provide a book, or offer ongoing support, your gift can help make encouragement, understanding, and connection more accessible.

Thank you for helping others find support on their journey toward healing.
