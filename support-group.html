About Our Support Group

Understanding, hope, and healing for ladies navigating narcissistic and emotional abuse, unhealthy relationship patterns, and attachment wounds.

You do not have to walk this journey alone

A place to be understood

Heart to Heart is a support group for ladies healing from narcissistic and emotional abuse, unhealthy relationship patterns, and attachment wounds. Led by Sherée Bergman, the group brings together education, peer support, encouragement, and coaching-based guidance in a compassionate, faith-centered community.

If a difficult relationship has left you feeling confused, unheard, emotionally exhausted, or unsure of yourself, you are welcome here. This is a place to learn, share, listen, grow, and take steps toward healthier relationships.

Who is this group for?

You may still be trying to understand a relationship, have recently left, or be working through experiences from years ago. There is no expectation that everyone will be at the same place in their journey.

Perhaps you find yourself asking, “Was it really abuse?” “Why do I keep questioning myself?” or “How can I trust again?” You can bring those questions with you. You do not need to have all the answers before you come.

Understanding the patterns

Our conversations explore narcissistic and emotional abuse alongside attachment wounds and unhealthy relationship patterns. We make room to talk about confusing experiences, boundaries, self-trust, and healthier ways of relating.

Recognizing patterns is not about blaming yourself for what happened. It is about gaining understanding so that the past does not have to determine your future.

What can I expect at a meeting?

Meetings offer teaching, encouragement, and opportunities to learn, share, and listen alongside others who understand. You are welcome to participate at your own pace; there is no expectation that you tell your story or share more than you feel comfortable sharing.

Morning and evening sessions offer identical teachings, so you can choose the session that works best for you. Check the Group Sessions page for the current dates, times, timezone, and location before attending.

Topics we explore

  • Understanding narcissistic and emotional abuse
  • Attachment wounds and recurring relationship patterns
  • Setting and maintaining healthy boundaries
  • Recognizing emotional triggers
  • Rebuilding self-trust and confidence
  • Healthy communication and healthier connections
  • Grief, forgiveness, faith, and personal growth

Faith, encouragement, and hope

Christian faith, encouragement, and a relationship with God are central to Sherée’s approach to healing. However, no one will be turned away.

The group offers space for the emotional, relational, physical, and spiritual aspects of healing. Sherée brings her experience as a coach, Ordained Minister, and Chaplain to her work. Read Sherée’s story and professional background.

Respect for your story and privacy

Members are asked to respect one another and protect other members’ identities, stories, and personal circumstances. Before attending, please read the Support Group Confidentiality and Disclaimer Agreement, which explains the group’s expectations, recording restrictions, leader guidance, and limits of confidentiality.

You decide what you feel comfortable sharing. Listening is a meaningful way to participate, too.

Session fees and sponsorship

A $10 fee each time you attend a session helps cover room rental, setup, and other support-group expenses. Book costs are separate.

If these costs are difficult for you to manage, please speak with Sherée privately. Someone on our sponsorship list may be able to help.

To sponsor someone’s session fees or book costs, contact Sherée directly or leave your sponsorship gift in the designated box at the entry of the meeting room.

How to join us

  1. Visit Group Sessions and choose a morning or evening meeting.
  2. Click “RSVP for this session” and enter your name and email address.
  3. Look for your confirmation email with the session details.

On the RSVP form, you can choose a reminder about 24 hours before your reserved session. You can also subscribe to separate upcoming-event emails one week before each meeting date, with morning and evening details and an RSVP link. Confirm your email to activate that subscription, and unsubscribe anytime using the link in those emails.

If you would like to ask a question before taking the first step, contact Sherée or visit our frequently asked questions.

Heart to Heart support groups provide education, peer support, encouragement, and coaching-based guidance. They are not psychotherapy or a substitute for diagnosis or treatment from a licensed mental-health professional.
